Our commitment

Accessibility, broken down honestly

We build against the same four principles that define WCAG itself — not a marketing checklist.

P

Perceivable

Every color pairing on this site is verified at 6:1 to 17:1 contrast — well past the WCAG AA minimum of 4.5:1 — and text scales to 200% without breaking layout.

O

Operable

Every action has a keyboard path and a visible, high-contrast focus ring. Nothing in EchoNotes requires precise pointer control or a timed response.

U

Understandable

Plain-language labels, consistent navigation, and predictable behavior — screen-reader users always know what a control will do before activating it.
